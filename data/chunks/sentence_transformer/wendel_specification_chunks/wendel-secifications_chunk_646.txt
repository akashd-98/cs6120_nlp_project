part 2 - products 2. 1 pipe labels a. general requirements for manufactured pipe labels : preprinted, color - coded, with lettering indicating service, and showing flow direction. b. pretensioned pipe labels : precoiled, semirigid plastic formed to cover full circumference of pipe and to attach to pipe without fasteners or adhesive. c. self - adhesive pipe labels : printed plastic with contact - type, permanent - adhesive backing. d. pipe label contents : include identification of piping service using same designations or abbreviations as used on drawings, pipe size, and an arrow indicating flow direction. 1. flow - direction arrows : integral with piping system service lettering to accommodate both directions, or as separate unit on each pipe label to indicate flow direction. 2. lettering size : at least 1 - 1 / 2 inches high. 2. 2 valve tags a. valve tags : stamped or engraved with 1 / 4 - inch letters for piping system abbreviation and 1 / 2 - inch numbers. 1. tag material : brass, 0. 032 - inch minimum thickness, and having predrilled or stamped holes for attachment hardware. 2. fasteners : brass wire - link or beaded chain ; or
