renovations – phase 2 wendel project no. 610101 section 088000 – glazing part 1 - general 1. 1 related documents a. drawings and general provisions of the contract, including general and supplementary conditions and division 01 specification sections, apply to this section. 1. 2 summary a. section includes glazing for the following products and applications, including those specified in other sections where glazing requirements are specified by reference to this section : 1. doors. 2. interior borrowed lites. 3. glazing sealants and accessories. 1. 3 definitions a. glass manufacturers : firms that produce primary glass, fabricated glass, or both, as defined in referenced glazing publications. b. glass thicknesses : indicated by thickness designations in millimeters according to astm c 1036. c. interspace : space between lites of an insulating - glass unit. 1. 4 coordination a. coordinate glazing channel dimensions to provide necessary bite on glass, minimum edge and face clearances, and adequate sealant thicknesses, with reasonable tolerances. 1. 5 performance requirements a. general : installed glazing systems shall withstand normal thermal movement and wind and impact loads ( where applicable ) without failure, including loss or glass breakage
